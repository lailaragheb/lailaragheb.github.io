About

Cairo · writing since 2023

I'm a bilingual UX writer and content designer in Cairo. I started writing professionally in 2023, first for AI products. For the past two years I've been building content at a digital bank in Egypt: UX copy for 20+ user journeys, a bilingual content design system, a knowledge base of 350+ tutorials, and tools that remove the repetitive parts of content work.

I also teach and hand over what I build: I onboarded a design team on the content system, trained a customer support team on the knowledge base and handed it to a junior writer, and presented Arabic voice options to a CEO.

Before writing, I spent almost two years in technical support at a software company. It's why I care about clear steps, honest error messages and the questions customers actually ask.

Open to senior content designer and senior UX writer roles, remote or in Cairo and the Gulf, and to freelance copywriting.
