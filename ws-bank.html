AI monitoring systems and how they can prevent bank robberies

Writing sample

Contrary to popular belief, banks don’t only provide services related to money, loans, and accounts. Main bank branches also house vaults that hold everything from important government documents and records to diamonds, gold, and cash.

Even though vaults are thought to be impossible to break into, unfortunately, that isn’t always true. This brings us to the story of the Antwerp diamond heist, a heist that was named “The heist of the century” because it was the largest ever diamond heist and one of the largest robberies in history.

During the weekend of 15–16 February 2003, in the Antwerp diamond district, a group of thieves successfully stole loose diamonds, gold, silver and other types of jewelry valued at more than $100 million. Like banks, vaults also exist in many other sectors, including diamond trading centers. The vault that held the diamonds was said to be two floors below the main floor. It was protected by multiple security mechanisms, including a lock with 100 million possible combinations, infrared heat detectors, a seismic sensor, Doppler radar, and a magnetic field. The building itself had a private security force and was located in the heavily guarded and monitored Antwerp diamond district.

People call this heist “The flawless heist” because to this day it remains a mystery how the thieves were able to enter without triggering the security systems. However, looking at the available information, we can make some educated guesses about how they pulled off this perfect heist.

  • The robbery required eighteen months of preparation.
  • Leonardo Notarbartolo, one of the thieves, had rented a sparsely furnished office in the Antwerp World Diamond Centre to be able to pose as an Italian diamond merchant to gain credibility.
  • The group used camera pens to take covert pictures of the Centre and the vault during Notarbartolo's frequent visits.
  • They were able to plant a small hidden camera above the vault door. It would observe guards opening the door and record the combination they used.

This group of professional thieves, known as “The School of Turin,” was able to pull off the impossible, and did it without violence. This heist single-handedly threatened Antwerp’s reputation as the hub of the world diamond trade.

Could AI have detected a crime such as the Antwerp diamond heist?

The Antwerp diamond heist wasn’t only a wake-up call for the banking industry and other sectors that mainly depend on vaults to safely store their valuables, but it also showed customers and stakeholders that even the most secure vaults are vulnerable, and that their valuable possessions might not be as safe as they thought. So, what measures could banks take to prevent such crimes from happening? The answer to this question, like many others nowadays, is AI.

Before we talk about all the cool AI features that could have been implemented to prevent such a crime, let’s talk more about vulnerabilities in the diamond heist plan that AI could have detected. First, Leonardo Notarbartolo relied heavily on a false identity to orchestrate his perfect plan. He posed as an Italian diamond merchant who didn’t exist. However, customers’ identities are among the easiest things for AI to verify. All the center had to do was run background checks to make sure this person was who he said he was, and leave the rest to AI. This way, he wouldn’t have been able to access the center so frequently and gather this much information in the months leading up to the heist.

The second thing AI could have prevented is the small hidden camera that the group planted above the vault door. A simple AI object detection feature could have spotted a strange object within seconds. In addition, AI can now detect abnormal behavior, so the group could have been caught trying to plant the device, no matter how subtle and discreet they were.

Now that we know the vulnerabilities in this heist’s plan, how exactly can we implement all these cool AI features to prevent such crimes?

How can AI protect vaults against robberies?

An increasing number of banks and other businesses are starting to use AI to safeguard their assets and deter crime, as these systems become more sophisticated and more affordable. Since AI technology is always evolving, we can expect it to become even more crucial in preventing robberies like the Antwerp heist.

A standard CCTV security camera can become a smart, AI-powered surveillance system by adding an AI processor, which can help prevent bank crimes and strengthen vault security by:

  • Identifying patterns and anomalies. Unlike human security workers, AI systems can examine vast volumes of data from sensors, surveillance cameras, and other sources to spot trends and abnormalities that might point to suspicious activity. An AI system can easily identify, for instance, a pattern of unusual behavior by a specific employee, or notice something odd in the transfer of goods inside the vault.
  • Detecting and responding to threats in real time. AI monitoring systems can instantly detect unauthorized intrusion, non-permitted access, and other security threats, and alert security personnel so they can prevent potential crimes. For example, an AI system can detect a break-in attempt and immediately alert security personnel so they can respond quickly.
  • Adapting to new threats. Since criminals are constantly developing new methods of attack, it is important to stay ahead by identifying and responding to new threats as they emerge. AI systems can use historical data to learn and adapt to new threats over time.
  • Using facial recognition features. AI can analyze security camera footage and help identify known suspects, even when they wear masks or disguises.
  • Behavioral analysis. AI can detect abnormal behavior, such as running, loitering, falling, and fighting, and automatically send alerts to security personnel for early threat detection and faster, proactive intervention.
  • Intrusion detection systems driven by AI. AI monitoring systems can detect and report any movement in restricted areas, unwanted intrusion, and non-permitted access. Combined with facial recognition, they can also verify authorized users.

Take initiative and stay ahead of the game!

Vault safety is becoming increasingly important in the banking sector, as banks are starting to store very sensitive documents and valuables in them. If a bank gets a reputation for having unsafe vaults, it can have a significant impact on both banks and their customers. However, there are several things that can be done to prevent these crimes, and AI can play a major role in this effort.

AI-powered security monitoring systems can help banks predict and prevent robberies, detect and respond to robberies in real time, identify and track suspects, and increase the visibility and awareness of security personnel.

By using AI, banks can make themselves less attractive targets for criminals and better protect their customers and employees.
